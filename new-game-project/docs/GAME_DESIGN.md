# Game Design
**Status: PROVISIONAL WORKING DRAFT**

An RPG influenced by old MUDs and modern roguelites. Traits develop through exploration, choices, and turn-based combat. Death is a progression mechanic: the circumstances or source of death can grant a predetermined persistent bonus to future runs.

Prototype goal: a short playable loop with multiple runs and several distinct death bonuses. Exploration, atmospheric rooms, discovery, and meaningful uses for all six traits matter. Not every room should be combat or a trap.

Working traits: Puissant (Strength), Alacrity (Dexterity), Measure (Constitution), Aptitude (technical/learned understanding), Acumen (awareness/interpretation), Glamour (social and supernatural influence).

Combat timing, enemy telegraphs, armor, evasion, shields, wounds, trait formulas, and bonus stacking remain provisional or experimental until explicitly approved.
